## Flagship product: Lina (لينا)
- An AI receptionist and patient-relationship system for dental clinics that runs on WhatsApp: answers in seconds day and night (Arabic incl. Gulf dialect, English, Turkish), answers only from clinic-approved knowledge, books/reschedules into the clinic calendar, sends reminders and aftercare, follows up with patients who go quiet, brings patients back for check-ups, hands urgent/clinical/sensitive cases to the team with full context. Does not diagnose.
- **Business model (decided 2026-10-13):** sold as a system, not a subscription. Flowramo installs Lina on hosting the clinic chooses and owns, connected to the clinic's own accounts (WhatsApp, calendar, etc.). Patient data stays on the clinic's infrastructure.
- A patient self-service command to see and delete their stored data is planned by Habib (not built yet — do not claim it as live).

## Audience
1. Business owners and operators (clinics first, then service businesses in the Gulf, Türkiye and beyond) with repetitive, conversation-heavy or knowledge-heavy operations.
2. Dental clinic owners and managing doctors (Lina).
They are busy, sceptical of AI hype, care about revenue, reliability, privacy and control.

## Voice
Expert, calm, specific. Psychological persuasion with restraint: loss aversion and cost of inaction, specificity over adjectives, authority through craft and evidence, reciprocity through useful content, objection handling. No hype words, no exclamation marks, no fake urgency.
